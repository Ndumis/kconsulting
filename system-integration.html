FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our system integration service

What is system integration?

System integration connects your separate business tools and platforms so they share data and work together automatically. For example, integrating your website with your CRM means new leads flow directly into your client system — no manual data entry required. It eliminates the gaps between your tools and keeps your operations running smoothly.

Which business systems can you integrate?

We connect websites, CRM systems, WhatsApp Business, email platforms, scheduling tools, lead management systems, internal workflows, and more. If your business runs on multiple digital tools that don't communicate, we build the connections to unify them into a single, efficient operational system.

How long does a typical integration project take?

Simple integrations — such as connecting a website form to your CRM — can be completed in 1–2 weeks. More complex multi-system integrations with custom workflows typically take 4–8 weeks. We provide a clear scope and realistic timeline before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Will integration disrupt my existing business operations?

No — we design integrations to work alongside your existing setup. Most integrations are implemented without downtime and thoroughly tested before going live. We also provide support during the transition period to ensure everything runs smoothly from day one.
